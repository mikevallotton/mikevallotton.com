There's one AI mistake that instantly makes people feel like they don't get it, and it is avoidable.

Most people treat AI like a search engine. That makes sense. We've all been using search engines for a long time. They ask once, and if the answer isn't great, they stop.

They think, I must be doing this wrong. Maybe AI isn't for me, or this tool isn't good.

But AI is not a database. It's a collaborator. And the first answer is almost never the best answer. It's the starting point.

The real power comes from the next question. Refine this. Push this further. Explain the trade offs. Rewrite it at a higher level. What am I missing? Give me three alternatives. Simplify this.

When you treat AI as iterative, you stop feeling inadequate and you start feeling capable.

The mistake isn't getting a mediocre first answer. The mistake is stopping there.
