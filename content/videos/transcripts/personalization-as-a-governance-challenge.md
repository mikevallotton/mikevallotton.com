It’s pretty well established that personalization drives revenue, and increases it.

But there’s a tension that leaders wrestle with, is that the more precisely you tailor experiences, the more customers wonder how you know what you know.

And AI increases the ability to infer intent, but it also increases that opacity.

Customers are expecting relevance, but they also expect ethical boundaries. And if personalization feels invasive, performance drops. If governance slows innovation, then growth stalls.

So personalization is not just a marketing lever, it is a governance problem. And the companies that figure that balance out early are going to outperform the companies who don’t.
