So when everyone has powerful tools, what actually sets you apart?

When execution is easy, your competitive advantage shifts to the systems that organize and coordinate that execution.

If anyone can produce research and analysis and code, capability gaps shrink, and the advantage moves to how you integrate workflow systems, feedback loops, and decision-making processes.

Those systems are harder to copy, and that’s where the competitive advantage is.

If we think about two competing startups, they’re both using Claude Code. They’re both using the same skills. Who wins?

It’s the one with the better process.
