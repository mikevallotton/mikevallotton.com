So I hope you’re getting some use out of the News Investigator, or at least thought that the prompt was educational.

But none of this was really about the news. This isn’t a news channel. It’s about collecting the information you need to make good decisions. The goal is not to outsource your thinking to AI. It’s to build better judgment by giving yourself better information to think with.

Right now AI-embedded glasses aren’t common, and direct neural interfaces are still experimental, but that doesn’t mean human augmentation isn’t already here. One of the most practical ways to become a more capable person today is to use AI to challenge your assumptions, expose yourself to different perspectives, and build a more accurate mental model of how the world works.

Those principles apply to current events, but they also apply to business decisions, technical problems, financial decisions, major purchases, really anything where better information leads to better judgment.

And that’s what I mean when I talk about AI augmenting people, not replacing your thinking, improving it.
