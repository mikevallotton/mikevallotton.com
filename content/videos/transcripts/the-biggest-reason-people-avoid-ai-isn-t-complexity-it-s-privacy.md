The biggest reason I see people not using AI is privacy concerns. And honestly, that's very reasonable.

By default, most AI tools log your conversations. They store what you type. Many of them are using your conversations to train future versions of those models. That's how the defaults work.

The good news, though, is that you have options. You can turn on no training or zero retention modes. You can use enterprise accounts that don't train on your data. Or, if it's very private, you can run a local LLM, and none of the information ever leaves your device.
