AI doesn’t think. Large language models predict the next token, based on the tokens that came before.

If you ask an LLM about something that’s political, controversial, or still developing, and you don’t give it enough context, you’re asking it to fill in the gaps. It will confidently generate something that is not well supported.

Most people think prompt engineering is about finding the perfect sentence, but most of the time it’s about giving the model the right context and enough structure to do the job well.

That’s why news investigators spend so much effort collecting information from multiple sources, separating facts from opinions, identifying uncertainty, and organizing information before the model even starts generating an answer.

The goal isn’t to tell the model what to think. It’s to give it enough information to reason over. The better the context, the better the output, and that’s a principle that applies far beyond just this agent.
