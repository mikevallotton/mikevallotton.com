# Test Case 05: Local / Small-Employer Seeker

## Scenario
Small local business with relatively limited public information.

## Target Company For Full Workflow
Books in the City / We Are LIT, Grand Rapids, MI

## Synthetic User Profile Input
Name: Marcus Thompson

Location: Grand Rapids, MI. Wants local in-person or hybrid work. Not interested in relocating.

Current situation: Looking for a stable local role connected to books, community programming, retail operations, libraries, education, or neighborhood businesses.

Experience:
- 6 years in retail and community-facing work.
- Assistant manager at a local gift shop for three years; handled opening/closing, inventory counts, vendor ordering, customer service, event setup, and social media posts.
- Part-time circulation assistant at a branch library for two years; helped patrons find materials, supported children's programming logistics, and handled basic catalog/account questions.
- Volunteer coordinator for a neighborhood arts festival; recruited 35 volunteers, built shift schedules, and coordinated check-in.
- Runs a small Instagram account reviewing books by Michigan authors; 1,800 followers.
- Has used Shopify, Square, Canva, Mailchimp, Instagram, Facebook Pages, Google Sheets, and basic inventory spreadsheets.

Accomplishments:
- Reduced stockout issues at the gift shop by creating a weekly reorder checklist.
- Helped grow festival volunteer retention by sending post-event thank-you notes and early sign-up reminders.
- Organized two local-author display tables at the library.
- Built a simple email newsletter for a community group that reached 600 subscribers.

Interests:
- Independent bookstores.
- Local author events.
- Multicultural literature.
- Community programming.
- Retail operations.
- Helping small businesses look more organized online.

Constraints:
- Salary target: $45k to $60k full-time, or part-time equivalent if paired with other work.
- Needs predictable scheduling.
- Comfortable with some evenings/weekends for events, but not every weekend.
- Prefers mission-driven local businesses over large chains.

## Golden Expectations
- The workflow should handle thin public information without inventing scale, hiring needs, or financial condition.
- Prompt 2 should include local businesses and community organizations, not only companies with formal job postings.
- Company research should cite official pages and clearly label community/market context as inference.
- Overlap should identify possible value around retail operations, events, local author programming, email/social, and inventory, but should avoid assuming the business can hire.
- Approach planning should emphasize low-pressure local engagement, checking mutual community ties, attending public events, and offering specific help only if appropriate.
