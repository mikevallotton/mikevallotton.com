## Scenario
Company initially appears relevant but becomes a weak or narrow match.

## Target Company For Full Workflow
Duolingo

Education and credentials:

Accomplishments:

Interests:

- Curriculum quality.
- AI-assisted content creation with human review.
- Learner motivation.
- Assessment design.

Constraints:
- Needs compensation of at least $75k.
- Cannot relocate outside Pittsburgh.
- Needs a transition path that values teaching experience.
- Not ready for software engineering, data science, product management, or senior learning science roles.

## Golden Expectations
- The workflow should recognize why Duolingo looks tempting but may be a weak fit for immediate employment.
- The profile should distinguish classroom teaching evidence from software-company evidence.
- Company research should surface Duolingo's AI, experimentation, curriculum, and learning-science orientation.
- Overlap should be skeptical and may recommend research more or deprioritize unless a role clearly values classroom curriculum experience.
- Approach planning should avoid telling Sofia to pitch core product or AI strategy. It should focus on learning from relevant roles, strengthening proof, and watching for narrow-fit openings.
