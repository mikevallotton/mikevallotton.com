# Test Case 04: Broad Generalist

## Scenario
Company with ambiguous fit.

## Target Company For Full Workflow
Zapier

## Synthetic User Profile Input
Name: Priya Nair

Location: Austin, TX. Remote-first preference. Open to occasional travel.

Current situation: Broad startup generalist trying to explain her value without sounding unfocused. She wants a role in operations, partner programs, content operations, automation enablement, or community-led growth.

Experience:
- 8 years across startup operations, founder support, content operations, and community programs.
- First hire at a 12-person bootstrapped education technology company; handled customer support, onboarding webinars, billing cleanup, vendor coordination, hiring logistics, and weekly KPI reporting.
- Operations manager at a 60-person creator tools startup; managed content calendar operations, freelancer onboarding, customer webinar logistics, and CRM hygiene.
- Built Airtable systems for editorial workflow, partner tracking, sponsor deliverables, and customer interview notes.
- Built 25+ Zapier automations connecting Typeform, Airtable, Slack, HubSpot, Stripe, Gmail, and Google Sheets.
- Created internal SOP library with 80+ process documents.
- Organized three small customer conferences with 120-250 attendees.
- Wrote practical guides for customers but has not owned a major brand or content strategy.

Accomplishments:
- Reduced manual invoice follow-up time by about 10 hours per week using automation.
- Increased webinar attendance rate from 34% to 48% by improving reminder workflows and follow-up segmentation.
- Built partner tracker adopted by sales and marketing teams.
- Helped founders prepare investor updates and board metrics.

Interests:
- No-code automation.
- Practical AI adoption.
- Partner ecosystems.
- Community education.
- Making messy processes usable.

Constraints:
- Salary target: $110k to $140k.
- Not interested in pure executive assistant roles.
- Not a software engineer.
- Wants a role with ownership, but needs the scope to be clearer than "do everything."

## Golden Expectations
- The profile should avoid presenting Priya as expert in everything.
- Prompt 2 should identify companies where generalist operations plus automation enablement is specifically useful.
- Zapier research should surface product, partner ecosystem, AI automation, remote culture, and customer education themes.
- Overlap should narrow Priya's possible value to specific functions rather than saying "she could help anywhere."
- Approach planning should prioritize partner enablement, customer education, operations, or community paths, and should warn against vague "I can help with operations" messaging.
