## Prompt Output

### 1. Approach Intensity
Observe first. Make one modest inquiry only after visiting, learning, and checking community ties.

### 2. Approach Thesis
Books in the City may be a strong local values/skills fit, but public evidence does not show hiring capacity. Marcus should learn respectfully before asking about work.

### 3. Most Relevant Problems Or Opportunities
Hypotheses only:
1. Retail/inventory support.
2. Local author or event logistics.
3. Newsletter/social consistency.

### 4. Research Targets
- Store website/newsletter.
- Public events or social posts.
- Studio Park/Arts Marketplace context.
- Local author/community contacts.
- GLIBA/public bookseller events.

### 5. Message Targets
- None immediately.
- After observation: owner/shop manager only for a short, modest inquiry, or a mutual community contact for advice.

### 6. Do-Not-Contact-Yet Targets
- Owner with a long unsolicited improvement plan.
- Customers or authors as referral targets.
- Staff during busy retail moments.
- Public social tags asking for work.

### 7. Ecosystem Paths To Check
Library coworkers, festival contacts, local authors, Michigan book Instagram network, Studio Park events, GLIBA public resources, Grand Rapids book clubs and arts organizations.

### 8. Recommended Engagement Plan
1. Visit as a customer and observe without pitching.
2. Subscribe to newsletter/check public events.
3. Prepare one-page proof packet.
4. Ask one mutual contact for advice if available.
5. Make one concise paid-work inquiry if appropriate.

### 9. First Message Goal
Validation: ask whether they ever need paid part-time or project help. Not a referral ask and not a diagnosis.

### 10. Message Angles
- "I am local to Grand Rapids and have retail, library, inventory, and community-event experience. I admire the book culture you are building and wanted to ask whether you ever need paid part-time or project help with retail operations, events, inventory, or customer communications."
- "I do not want to assume you are hiring. I am trying to learn whether there is a practical way someone with my local retail and book/community background could be useful."

### 11. Public Post Guidance
Do not make a public job-seeking post tagging the shop. A general post about visiting local bookstores or local authors is fine if authentic, but it should not pressure the business.

### 12. Evidence To Gather Before Contact
- Local one-page resume.
- Reorder checklist sample.
- Newsletter sample.
- Instagram/local author examples.
- Availability and compensation requirements.
- References.
- Do not use confidential employer, customer, library patron, volunteer, or proprietary data in any proof packet or outreach material.

### 13. Compensation and Boundary Notes
Do not offer unpaid operational labor as a path to a job. Volunteering at community events is separate from paid work and should not replace income. If the shop can only offer occasional hours, Marcus should keep adjacent employers active.

### 14. Fallback Targets
- Sourced fallback employers from Prompt 2: Schuler Books, Grand Rapids Public Library, Literacy Center of West Michigan, Arts Marketplace / Studio Park ecosystem.
- Additional local arts/retail venues with predictable schedules; source specific employers before outreach.

### 15. One-Week Action Plan
Day 1: Review website/newsletter/social/event info.  
Day 2: Prepare one-page resume and proof samples.  
Day 3: Visit as a customer.  
Day 4: Check mutual community contacts.  
Day 5: Source adjacent employer roles.  
Day 6: Make one inquiry only if appropriate.  
Day 7: Continue or move to fallback targets.

### 16. Stop Conditions
- No hiring capacity.
- Only unpaid work is available.
- Schedule/pay does not fit.
- Marcus feels tempted to diagnose the business from outside.
- Adjacent employers offer better stability.

### 17. Reusable Plan Summary
Approach Books in the City through observe-first local engagement, paid-work boundaries, and one modest inquiry. Keep larger bookstores, libraries, literacy nonprofits, and local arts employers active in parallel.
