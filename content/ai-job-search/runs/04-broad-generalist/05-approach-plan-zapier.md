## Prompt Output

### 1. Approach Intensity
Targeted research plus proof-building. Messaging is appropriate only after Priya has a small public/sanitized proof packet.

### 2. Approach Thesis
Zapier may value operators who can make AI automation understandable and usable for non-technical customers and partners. Priya's relevant value is customer education operations, not generic ops.

### 3. Most Relevant Problems Or Opportunities
1. Customer education for AI automation.
2. Webinar/template/content operations.
3. Partner enablement operations.

### 4. Research Targets
- Customer education/lifecycle education teams.
- Zapier events/webinars.
- Partner Hub and partner enablement.
- Content/template operations.
- Current jobs board and talent community.

### 5. Message Targets
- At most 2 people in customer education or partner enablement after proof packet is ready.

### 6. Do-Not-Contact-Yet Targets
- CEO/founders.
- Senior channel partnership leaders for referral asks.
- Engineering/API leaders.
- Customers featured in case studies.

### 7. Ecosystem Paths To Check
No-code communities, former coworkers using Zapier, Zapier partner apps she has worked with, webinar speakers, Austin/remote startup ops communities. Check for real relationships first.

### 8. Recommended Engagement Plan
1. Create proof packet: automation case studies, webinar workflow, partner tracker map, SOP sample.
2. Publish or prepare one automation teardown that is useful even if no one hires her.
3. Scan roles for customer education operations and partner enablement operations.
4. Send one or two learning notes only after team language is clear.

### 9. First Message Goal
Validation: learn whether Zapier has operations roles inside customer education or partner enablement.

### 10. Message Angles
- "I have built no-code automation systems for webinars, partner tracking, content operations, and customer workflows. I am trying to understand whether customer education operations at Zapier uses operators who turn automation examples into repeatable programs."
- "My strongest lane is where automation, documentation, and program operations meet. I would value advice on which teams own that work at Zapier."

### 11. Public Post Guidance
A public post can make sense if it is a useful automation teardown or workflow lesson, not a "please hire me" post. It should demonstrate judgment and practical examples.

### 12. Evidence To Gather Before Contact
- Sanitized Zap/Airtable examples.
- Impact metrics.
- Webinar workflow.
- Partner tracker process map.
- Public automation teardown.
- Do not use confidential employer, customer, partner, or proprietary data in any proof packet, teardown, or outreach material.

### 13. Compensation and Boundary Notes
Salary target is plausible for mid-level ops/enablement roles but less likely for junior content/community roles. Avoid unpaid consulting or building custom automations for companies during informal conversations.

### 14. Fallback Targets
- Sourced fallback employers from Prompt 2: Airtable, HubSpot, Notion, Webflow.
- Mid-sized automation or RevOps tooling companies.
- Source specific mid-sized automation or RevOps tooling employers before outreach.

### 15. One-Week Action Plan
Day 1: Pick customer education operations as primary lane.  
Day 2: Draft two automation case studies.  
Day 3: Create webinar workflow case study.  
Day 4: Scan roles and team language.  
Day 5: Draft public teardown.  
Day 6: Message one relevant person if proof is ready.  
Day 7: Apply only to a matching role or broaden fallback targets.

### 16. Stop Conditions
- No matching role evidence after one week.
- Roles require engineering/API, product marketing ownership, or senior channel revenue strategy.
- Priya cannot produce proof examples.
- Conversations stay too vague.

### 17. Reusable Plan Summary
Approach Zapier through customer education operations for AI automation. Build proof first, message sparingly, use public posting only as a useful teardown, and pursue fallback customer education/partner ops targets in parallel.
